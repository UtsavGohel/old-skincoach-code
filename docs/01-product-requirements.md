# Overview

SkinCoach is an AI-powered mobile application that helps users improve their skin health through daily AI-powered facial analysis, personalized skincare coaching, routine tracking, and long-term progress monitoring.

Unlike traditional AI skin scanner apps that provide one-time reports, SkinCoach focuses on continuous improvement by comparing scans over time and providing personalized recommendations based on trends and user habits.

The product aims to become a user's daily skincare companion.

---

# Vision

Become the world's most trusted AI-powered Personal Skin Health Coach.

Instead of simply analyzing skin, SkinCoach helps users understand why their skin changes, build healthier skincare habits, and measure real progress over weeks and months.

---

# Problem Statement

Millions of people spend money every month on skincare products, dermatologists, and cosmetic treatments without knowing:

- Is my skin actually improving?
- Is this product working?
- Why did I suddenly break out?
- Which habits affect my skin the most?
- What should I change?

Existing apps usually perform one-time AI scans and offer generic advice.

They fail to encourage long-term engagement or provide meaningful progress tracking.

---

# Solution

SkinCoach combines AI vision, daily tracking, and personalized coaching to create a continuous skin health experience.

Users simply take one selfie per day.

AI analyzes:

- Acne
- Redness
- Hydration
- Texture
- Pigmentation
- Oiliness
- Pores
- Wrinkles
- Dark Circles

The app compares today's scan with previous scans and generates personalized insights.
